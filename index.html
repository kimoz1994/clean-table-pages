How it works

  1. Click the extension icon on any page with a table (or right-click the table).
  2. If there is more than one table, pick yours from a simple list.
  3. See the cleaned preview — page junk removed, columns fixed, text unglued.
  4. Tick any column to adjust its cleaning, then download .xlsx or .csv.

What cleaning fixes

  • Floating headers and repeated header rows that duplicate in the middle of your file
  • Sort arrows and page decorations stuck to column names
  • Text glued together across blocks (the famous "ArsenalArsenalArsenal")
  • Empty filler columns and legend/keyword noise tables that aren't real tables

The trust point: you confirm before anything downloads

The preview shows exactly the rows and columns that will land in your file. Nothing leaves your browser and nothing downloads until you say so.

What it will not do (we'd rather be honest)

  • Tables that lazy-load more rows as you scroll: scroll the table fully first, then grab.
  • Very unusual layouts are not grabbed at all — the extension tells you instead of exporting junk.

Privacy

The extension runs entirely in your browser. Table data goes from the page to your download — our servers never receive it. No account, no tracking, no analytics. Full details in the privacy policy.
