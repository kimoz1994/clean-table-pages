Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

The short version

Tidy Table — a browser extension operated by Minidevs — collects nothing. Your table data goes from the web page straight to the download file you requested, inside your browser. It never passes through our servers.

What the extension does

When you click the extension on a page, it reads that page to find tables, shows you a preview inside the extension, and on your confirmation saves an .xlsx or .csv file to your computer. We do not run any analytics inside the extension, we have no accounts, and we receive no data about you.

Local storage

The extension remembers small settings in your browser's own local storage — for example your cleaning choices for specific columns. This stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

Pro purchases

The Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase, handled by Paddle (our payment provider). Paddle processes the payment as the merchant of record, so if you buy Pro, Paddle receives your email and payment details under Paddle's privacy policy. We never receive your payment details. The extension may contact Paddle's license service only to confirm that a license key is valid — no table data is involved in that check.

The website you are on

This website does not use cookies, trackers, ads, or analytics of any kind.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated date. Claims in it are kept deliberately small so we can keep them true.
